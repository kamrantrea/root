# Genesis (בְּרֵאשִׁית)

Genesis is the first book of the Torah and the Hebrew Bible. The Hebrew title *Bereshit* ("In the beginning") comes from the opening words of the text.

## Structure

- **Primeval History** (1–11): Creation, the Fall, Cain and Abel, Noah's Flood, Tower of Babel
- **Patriarchal Narratives** (12–50): Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph

## Key Themes

- Creation and cosmic order
- Covenant (with Noah, Abraham)
- Election and divine calling
