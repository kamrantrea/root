# Exodus (שְׁמוֹת)

Exodus is the second book of the Torah. Its Hebrew name *Shemot* ("Names") comes from its opening words listing the names of Jacob's sons.

## Structure

- **The Oppression and Moses** (1–6)
- **The Plagues and Exodus** (7–15)
- **Wilderness Wanderings** (16–18)
- **Covenant at Sinai** (19–24)
- **Tabernacle Instructions** (25–40)

## Key Themes

- Liberation and redemption
- Divine law (Ten Commandments)
- Covenant formation at Sinai
