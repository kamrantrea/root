# Isaiah (יְשַׁעְיָהוּ)

The Book of Isaiah contains some of the most sublime prophetic poetry in the Hebrew Bible. Scholars widely distinguish between at least two major sections:

## Sections

- **First Isaiah** (1–39): 8th-century BCE context, Assyrian threat
- **Deutero-Isaiah** (40–55): Babylonian exile context, comfort and restoration
- **Trito-Isaiah** (56–66): Post-exilic community

## Confidence Levels

| Claim | Level |
|-------|-------|
| Single author (traditional view) | Debated |
| Two or three distinct authors | Likely |
| Written during Babylonian exile (chs. 40–55) | Likely |
