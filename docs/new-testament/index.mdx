# New Testament

The New Testament is the second major division of the Christian biblical canon. It consists of 27 books written primarily in Koine Greek during the 1st century CE.

## Sections

- [Gospels](/library/new-testament/gospels/matthew) — Four accounts of Jesus's life
- [Epistles](/library/new-testament/epistles/romans) — Letters from Paul and others
