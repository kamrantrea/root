# Matthew (Κατὰ Ματθαῖον)

The Gospel of Matthew is the first book of the New Testament. It presents Jesus as the fulfillment of Hebrew prophecy and is often called the "Jewish Gospel."

## Structure

- **Infancy Narrative** (1–2)
- **Sermon on the Mount** (5–7)
- **Mission Discourse** (10)
- **Parables Discourse** (13)
- **Passion and Resurrection** (26–28)

## Confidence Levels

| Claim | Level |
|-------|-------|
| Written by apostle Matthew | Debated |
| Uses Mark as a source (Markan priority) | Likely |
| Composed c. 80–90 CE | Likely |
