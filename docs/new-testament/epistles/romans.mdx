# Romans (Πρὸς Ῥωμαίους)

The Epistle to the Romans is widely regarded as Paul's most systematic theological work. Written c. 56–58 CE, it addresses the Roman church he had not yet visited.

## Themes

- Righteousness of God
- Justification by faith
- Jew and Gentile in God's plan
- Ethics and life in the Spirit
