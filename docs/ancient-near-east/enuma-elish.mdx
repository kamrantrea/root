# Enuma Elish (𒂗𒌑𒈠𒂗𒆜)

The *Enuma Elish* is a Babylonian creation myth dating to the 18th–17th centuries BCE. It describes the creation of the world through a cosmic battle between Marduk and the chaos dragon Tiamat.

## Parallels with Genesis 1

- Watery chaos as primordial state
- Creation by division (firmament)
- Sequence: light, sky, land, luminaries, humans
